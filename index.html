ABOUT ME

I have worked at the intersection of data science, information technology, and research for over 25 years. I have a background in computer science and earned a doctorate in innovation and strategy from the University of Innsbruck. My work combines research with practical application. I regularly publish in both academic journals (1) and practice-oriented media (2, 3, 4, 5).

In market research, I focus on quantitative methods and use statistical models to analyze large data sets and identify patterns across groups. I also developed an AI-powered data analysis platform with interactive dashboards for large-scale studies.

In information technology, I design domain-specific languages (DSLs) and tools that automate specialized tasks. I also integrate large language models (LLMs) into domain workflows to support reasoning, user interaction, and decision-making.

I live with my family in Vorarlberg, Austria.

WHAT I DO
Market Research
IT & AI
Data Analysis

I use statistical modeling methods, including Choice-Based Conjoint, Kano analysis, and multivariate techniques, to analyze customer preferences and support data-driven decisions.


Online Data Gathering

I design online surveys for customers and employees, with a focus on clear structure, effective sampling, and high response quality.


Communication & Visualization

I communicate insights through clear data visualizations that make complex findings accessible and actionable for diverse audiences.


Web & Enterprise Software

I develop web and enterprise applications with Python/Django and Java/Spring/Vaadin. My work includes ORMs, application state management, and web components. I maintain MoWare Werkbank, a Java low-code platform (documentation).


Code Generation

I design logical abstractions and domain-specific languages using JetBrains MPS, with a focus on expressive tools and clear domain models.


LLM and AI Integration

I integrate large language models and AI agents into software systems and domain workflows. This includes connecting tools and data through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), developing agent skills and context engineering, and automating specialized tasks with validated agent workflows. I also build domain-specific LLM solutions with RAG and API integration.
